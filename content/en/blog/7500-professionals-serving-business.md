## About the Network

Founded in 1991 (35 years), In Extenso was built on the vision of uniting independent accountants with a shared goal: helping businesses grow.

Today, it's France's largest accounting network with nationwide presence. Unlike centralized big firms, each In Extenso agency is locally led by partners with deep territorial knowledge.
