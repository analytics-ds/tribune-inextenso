## Key Takeaways

1. Growth ≠ profitability. Companies can grow rapidly and become unprofitable
2. Three risks: underfunding (cash), understaffing (team), poor control (quality)
3. Key metrics: minimum cash balance, debt/revenue ratio, gross margin
4. Timing: restructure at 15% growth, not 60%

## Three Growth Pitfalls

### Pitfall 1: Underfunding (Cash Crunch)

50% more orders. Pay suppliers at 30 days, customers at 45 days.

**Example**:
- Revenue: 1M€ → 1.5M€
- Customer receivables tie-up: 187k€ (vs 125k€)
- Supplier payables: 125k€ (vs 83k€)
- Additional funding need: 62k€

If reserves are only 20k€, you're in crisis.

**Solution**: Finance 3-4 months before expansion.

### Pitfall 2: Understaffing (Team Burnout)

100% more customers, team unchanged.

**Warning signs**:
- Regular overtime (> 5h/week)
- Turnover > 15% annually
- Rising sick leave
- Longer response times

**Solution**: Recruit before saturation.

### Pitfall 3: Quality Decline

Unchanged processes, higher volume.

**Example**: Workshop with 5 staff scaled to 15. Without documented quality control, you get rejects, unhappy customers, reputation damage.

**Solution**: Systematize processes before scaling.

## Growth Management Metrics (3 Key Indicators)

### 1. Minimum Cash Balance (Weekly)

Don't drop below 2-3 weeks of operations.

If approaching minimum: reduce purchases or accelerate collections.

### 2. Debt/Revenue Ratio (Monthly)

Target: < 40% for SMEs

Above = over-leveraged and vulnerable.

### 3. Gross Margin (Monthly)

Target: maintain or improve

If margin drops while revenue rises: danger (selling cheaper to grow).

| Metric | Target | Frequency | Action |
|--------|--------|-----------|--------|
| Min cash | 2-3 weeks | Weekly | Reduce purchases or accelerate collections |
| Debt/Revenue | < 40% | Monthly | Repay debt or slow growth |
| Gross margin | Stable/↑ | Monthly | Raise prices or reduce costs |

## Well-Managed Growth Example

**Start**: Services PME, 10 staff, 2M€ revenue, 35% margin

**Goal**: 3M€ in 18 months (+50%)

**Financing Plan**:
- Self-funding (reinvested profits): 200k€
- Credit line: 150k€
- Owner contributions: 100k€
- **Total**: 450k€

**Expansion Plan**:
- Months 1-3: increase capacity (equipment, space)
- Months 2-6: recruit 5 staff progressively
- Months 1-18: test new clients without margin sacrifice
- Months 10-12: evaluate and adjust pricing

**Monitoring**:
- Cash: weekly (minimum 100k€)
- Debt: monthly (max 1.2M€)
- Margin: monthly (target 35-37%)

**Result**: 3M€ reached in 20 months (vs 18 planned), stable margin, stable team.

## Five Sustainability Rituals

1. **Monthly management review**
   - Cash, debt, margin, customer satisfaction
   - Adjust if metrics deviate

2. **Annual investment plan**
   - Machines, space, equipment, training
   - Pre-financed (no surprises)

3. **Team monitoring**
   - Regular 1:1s
   - Prevent turnover

4. **Process review**
   - Quarterly: what to improve for +10% growth?
   - Before problems, not after

5. **Profitability by sector/client**
   - Some clients cost more to serve
   - Some product lines consume margin

## Conclusion

Sustaining growth means anticipating. Companies that die in growth miss cash, team, or quality problems until too late.

Key timing: restructure at 15% growth, not 60%. Less painful and market will wait.
