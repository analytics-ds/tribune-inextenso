## Key Takeaways

1. Three regimes: exemption (< 85k€), simplified (85k€-250k€), normal (> 250k€)
2. VAT owed = Collected - Deductible. Monthly or quarterly filing depending on regime
3. E-invoicing improves accuracy (fewer VAT errors)
4. Common oversights: non-deductible VAT, forgotten stock, missing supplier invoices

## VAT Regimes

### Exemption (Microenterprises)

**Condition**: Revenue < 85,000 euros (services) or < 170,000 (goods)

**Advantage**: no VAT to declare or pay

**Disadvantage**: cannot recover VAT on purchases

### Simplified Regime

**Condition**: Revenue between 85k€ and 250k€

**Benefits**: fewer filings than normal regime

### Normal Regime

**Condition**: Revenue > 250,000 euros

**Benefits**: faster VAT recovery, detailed tracking

## Simple Calculation

VAT Collected = Invoiced amounts to customers
VAT Deductible = VAT paid to suppliers
VAT Due = VAT Collected - VAT Deductible

## Conclusion

Understanding VAT is key for compliance and proper financial management. Choose the right regime, use certified software, and work with your accountant for declarations.
