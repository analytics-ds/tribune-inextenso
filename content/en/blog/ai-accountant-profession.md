## Key Takeaways

1. AI will automate 60-70% of repetitive tasks (data entry, reconciliation, routine declarations)
2. New professions: AI consultant, data accountant, predictive analyst, strategic advisor
3. Human expertise becomes more valuable: judgment, client advice, crisis management
4. Training: accountants must learn AI tools and develop strategic advisory

## Tasks Threatened by Automation

Data entry will disappear progressively. With OCR and mandatory e-invoicing, documents are auto-extracted and pre-validated.

**Before 2024**: accountants manually enter 500 lines daily
**2024-2026**: AI pre-fills 90%, accountant validates
**2027-2028**: 99% automated

## Emerging New Professions

### 1. Data Accountant

Exploit company data for real-time optimization recommendations.

### 2. Tax Optimization Consultant

Use predictive tools to show clients where they overpay taxes.

### 3. Predictive Management Manager

Deploy intelligent dashboards predicting treasury crises 60 days ahead.

### 4. Accounting Risk Analyst

Detect anomalies and potential fraud before audit.

## AI Augments, Not Replaces

Where AI will fail:
- Strategic advising
- Client conflict management
- Value creation
- Ethical decisions

These require human judgment and experience.

## Pragmatic Transition

### For Accountants

1. Invest in AI-powered software (Sage, Ciel, Tiime, etc.)
2. Train teams on AI tools interpretation
3. Offer advisory (predictive audit, tax optimization)
4. Become advisor, not just bookkeeper

### For SME Leaders

- Move to AI-enabled providers in 2025-2026
- Ask your accountant if they use embedded AI
- Demand predictive management (not just reporting)
- Expect stable fees (less manual work)

## Conclusion

AI is not a threat to accounting, it's an opportunity. Accountants who stay focused on manual data entry will disappear. Those who evolve into strategic consultants with AI tools will thrive.

For you as a leader: the era of annual balance sheets is over. Demand real-time advice, predictive management, optimization. That's what AI pays for.
