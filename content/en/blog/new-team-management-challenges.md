## Key Takeaways

1. Modern management challenges: retention (up 30% turnover in SMEs since 2024), remote work, search for meaning
2. Common mistakes: micromanagement at distance, lack of regular feedback, non-competitive salaries
3. Key lever: autonomy granted + regular feedback + transparency on strategy
4. Tools: monthly 1:1 meetings, collaborative tools, annual satisfaction surveys

## Management Challenges in Flux

### Turnover Accelerates

In 2024, French SMEs lose an average of 25-30% of staff annually. Main reasons:
- Stagnant salaries (inflation not compensated)
- Lack of development prospects
- Poor management
- Search for meaning (employees want to work for aligned companies)

For leaders, high turnover is expensive: recruitment, training, productivity loss.

### Remote Work Creates New Demands

Teams are no longer in the same place. Risks:
- Sense of isolation
- Lack of spontaneity in exchanges
- Fragmented team cohesion
- Knowledge transfer difficulties

Best practices: regular synchronous moments, explicit collaboration tools, clear expectations.

### Gen Z Redefines Engagement

Born with the internet, Gen Z expects:
- Autonomy in execution (no micromanagement)
- Regular and honest feedback
- Alignment between stated and actual practices
- Rapid development (courses, internal mobility)

## Three Levers of Effective Leadership

### 1. Autonomy + Accountability

Set clear objectives, provide resources, freedom on how. Then regularly ask for accountability on results.

Common pitfall: granting autonomy but lacking follow-up, leading to feelings of abandonment.

### 2. Regular Feedback

Don't wait for annual evaluations. Useful feedback is:
- **Immediate**: a few days after the event
- **Specific**: "Yesterday's client presentation lacked clarity on phase 3"
- **Two-way**: listen to the employee's perspective

### 3. Strategy Transparency

An employee who understands the "why" stays longer. If your company targets 20% growth, explain how their work contributes.

## Tools and Rituals

**Monthly 1:1 meetings** (30-60 min):
- Results review
- Obstacle discussion
- Personal feedback
- Development perspectives

**Satisfaction survey** (1-2x yearly):
- Anonymous and honest
- Simple questions: engagement, salary fairness, future outlook
- Action plan based on results

**Collective moments** (even hybrid):
- 1 day together per quarter minimum
- Co-production workshops
- Victory celebrations

## Conclusion

Modern management challenges require more intentionality: structured autonomy, regular feedback, transparency. Companies mastering these three levers retain talent and build strong culture.

To start: have a 1:1 with each team member this month. Listen without judgment. You'll learn much.
